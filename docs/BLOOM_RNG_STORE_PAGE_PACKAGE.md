# Bloom RNG — Roblox Store Page Package

This package is intentionally limited to claims that are already represented by the v1 release manifest and current runtime source. Do not advertise unverified live-player counts, performance targets, or features that are still runtime-only QA gates.

## Experience title

**Bloom RNG**

## Short description

Roll rare blooms, grow a powerful garden, collect bees, unlock new sectors, and build permanent progression through Seasons and Legacy.

## Full description

🌸 **Roll for rare blooms and build your garden.**  
🐝 **Collect and equip bees** with distinct traits and abilities.  
🌿 Plant flowers, trigger synergies, raise mastery, and strengthen your Hive.  
🗺️ Unlock **Sunny Orchard, Crystal Greenhouse, and Twilight Grove** as your progression expands.  
⬡ Spend upgrades in a full-screen **Workshop hex tree** covering rolls, gardens, bees, economy, Bloom, exploration, and Legacy.  
🏆 Complete quests, world activities, trials, and Seasons to earn long-term progression.

**Bloom RNG** is a progression-focused Roblox simulator built around rolling, gardening, bees, exploration, and layered upgrades.

## Icon creative brief

Use one instantly readable focal subject. Center a luminous stylized bloom with one small bee orbiting it. Keep the background dark navy with cyan-blue rim light and restrained gold progression accents. The bloom should occupy most of the square and stay readable at tiny sizes. No small text, no fake UI, no item count claims.

Suggested visual hierarchy:

1. glowing bloom;
2. bee silhouette / trail;
3. subtle hexagonal Workshop motif;
4. distant garden particles.

## Thumbnail 1 — Roll → Bloom

Show the vertical roulette as a secondary element, with a rare glowing flower landing in the center and the Bloom meter charged nearby. Keep the reward readable without making the screen look like a casino. Caption candidate: **ROLL. GROW. BLOOM.**

## Thumbnail 2 — Garden + Bees

Show a developed circular garden with visibly different flower sizes, bees moving between blooms, and the central Hive in the distance. Use Sunny Orchard lighting so the image reads warm and welcoming. Caption candidate: **BUILD YOUR GARDEN**

## Thumbnail 3 — Progression + Workshop

Split the composition naturally between a new sector in the world and the cyan/gold Workshop hex tree. Emphasize that progression opens more systems rather than showing unsupported rewards. Caption candidate: **UNLOCK MORE EVERY SEASON**

## Capture / export rules

- Do not show legacy Fruit content as shipping gameplay.
- Avoid tiny text and dense HUD clutter.

- Capture final screenshots from the release candidate only; do not use editor mock data as if it were live gameplay.
